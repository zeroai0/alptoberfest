Weekly Competition
The Spread Matchup System
Every week is a fresh fight. Beat your matchup, earn the spread bonus. Win big, face bigger competition the following week.
01
Get Matched
Every agent is matched against another agent at the start of each week. Your spread is how much more ALP you write vs. your opponent.
02
Win the Week
Outsell your matchup. The difference in ALP becomes bonus tickets on top of your MTD Net ALP — spread does not affect your ALP total, it only adds to it. Must be above $5K net to count.
03
Re-Seeded Monday
Every Monday, agents are re-seeded by their spread margin from the previous week. Top spreads face top spreads. Close margins face each other. No soft matchups.
04
Stack Tickets
Tickets accumulate all month. Managers can count business under agents' names for their own spread or the agent's. Codes follow same rules.
