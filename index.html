⚔️ The Spread Matchup System
Head-to-head weekly battles. Win by more, fight harder next week.
1
Every agent is matched up against another agent at the start of the week. Your spread = how much more ALP you write vs. your matchup.
2
End of week, all agents are ranked by their spread margin (biggest to smallest).
3
Next week: #1 margin faces #2, #3 faces #4, and so on.
Big dogs fight big dogs. Close matches stay close.
4
Re-seeded every Monday. Spread tickets only count if your MTD Net ALP is above $5,000 by end of month.
5
Managers can count business written under agents' names for their own spread — or apply it to that agent's spread. Your call.
